Two-transistor fuzz

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 13 holes wide by 7 strips (1.3 x 0.7 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to G from the top and holes numbered 1 to 13 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. Cut the copper at 4 holes: A7, B5, B10, E7. On the copper side left and right are mirrored, so count from the same corner. Twist a 3.5 mm drill bit by hand until the copper ring is gone, then hold the board to a light to make sure no sliver bridges the cut.
3. Fit 1 wire link (insulated solid-core wire, lying flat): B9 to C9.
4. R1 33k resistor (orange orange orange gold) from hole A1 to hole D1.
5. R2 100k resistor (brown black yellow gold) from hole B6 to hole E6.
6. R3 470 resistor (yellow violet brown gold) from hole A6 to hole A9 lying along the strip over the cut.
7. R4 8.2k resistor (grey red red gold) from hole A10 to hole E10.
8. C3 10nF film capacitor from hole E9 to hole G9.
9. Q1 BC549C (TO-92, pins CBE left to right): C at D2, B at E2, E at F2. Polarity check: flat face toward the left.
10. Q2 BC549C (TO-92, pins CBE left to right): C at E8, B at D8, E at C8. Polarity check: flat face toward the right.
11. C1 2.2µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole E4, - at hole B4. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole B4.
12. C2 22µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole B13, - at hole F13. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole F13.
13. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: IN pad at hole B2 to the input jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); GND pad at hole F1 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground; +9V pad at hole A4 to the DC jack + (the sleeve lug on a 2.1 mm centre-negative jack) or the battery clip +; RV1 Fuzz pot (B1k, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to B7, lug 2 to B11, lug 3 to F11; RV2 Volume pot (A500k, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to G11, lug 2 to the output jack tip, lug 3 to F4.
14. Before power: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between +9V (A4) and GND (F1). It must not read near 0 ohms. Then check the neighbouring strips at every cut for solder bridges.

Notes:
- Nothing on the board touches net out: wire the output jack tip and RV2 lug 2 straight to each other.
