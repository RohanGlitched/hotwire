Common-emitter clean booster

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 9 holes wide by 7 strips (0.9 x 0.7 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to G from the top and holes numbered 1 to 9 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. Cut the copper at 1 hole: G4. On the copper side left and right are mirrored, so count from the same corner. Twist a 3.5 mm drill bit by hand until the copper ring is gone, then hold the board to a light to make sure no sliver bridges the cut.
3. RC 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole A1 to hole D1.
4. R2 82k resistor (grey red orange gold) from hole E4 to hole B4.
5. R1 470k resistor (yellow violet yellow gold) from hole A5 to hole E5.
6. RE 1k resistor (brown black red gold) from hole F7 to hole B7.
7. R3 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole G9 to hole B9.
8. C1 100nF film capacitor from hole G1 to hole E1.
9. Q1 2N3904 (TO-92, pins EBC left to right): E at F3, B at E3, C at D3. Polarity check: flat face toward the right.
10. CE 22µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole F6, - at hole B6. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole B6.
11. C2 1µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole D8, - at hole G8. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole G8.
12. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: IN pad at hole G2 to the input jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); +9V pad at hole A2 to the DC jack + (the sleeve lug on a 2.1 mm centre-negative jack) or the battery clip +; GND pad at hole B2 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground; OUT pad at hole G6 to the output jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass).
13. Before power: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between +9V (A2) and GND (B2). It must not read near 0 ohms. Then check the neighbouring strips at every cut for solder bridges.
