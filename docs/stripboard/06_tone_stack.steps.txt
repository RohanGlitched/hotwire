Three-knob tone stack

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 6 holes wide by 8 strips (0.6 x 0.8 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to H from the top and holes numbered 1 to 6 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. No track cuts are needed.
3. R1 100k resistor (brown black yellow gold) from hole A3 to hole D3.
4. R2 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole E5 to hole H5.
5. C1 250pF ceramic capacitor from hole A2 to hole C2.
6. C3 22nF film capacitor from hole D2 to hole F2.
7. C2 22nF film capacitor from hole D4 to hole B4.
8. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: GND pad at hole H1 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground; IN pad at hole A1 to the input jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); OUT pad at hole E1 to the output jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); RV1 Treble pot (A250k, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to C1, lug 2 to E3, lug 3 to B5; RV2 Bass pot (A1M, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to B1, lug 2 to F1, lug 3 to F1; RV3 Mid pot (B25k, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to F3, lug 2 to H2, lug 3 to H2.
9. Before connecting anything: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between neighbouring strips at every cut, and that IN and OUT do not read near 0 ohms to GND.

Notes:
- RV2 lugs 2 and 3 share a net: join them on the pot with a short wire and run one wire to the RV2·2+3 pad.
- RV3 lugs 2 and 3 share a net: join them on the pot with a short wire and run one wire to the RV3·2+3 pad.
