Inductor wah filter

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 16 holes wide by 10 strips (1.6 x 1.0 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to J from the top and holes numbered 1 to 16 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. Cut the copper at 4 holes: B9, C10, D6, F5. On the copper side left and right are mirrored, so count from the same corner. Twist a 3.5 mm drill bit by hand until the copper ring is gone, then hold the board to a light to make sure no sliver bridges the cut.
3. Fit 1 wire link (insulated solid-core wire, lying flat): D15 to B15.
4. R2 470k resistor (yellow violet yellow gold) from hole A1 to hole D1.
5. R1 68k resistor (blue grey orange gold) from hole F4 to hole J4.
6. R3 22k resistor (red red orange gold) from hole D5 to hole J5.
7. R5 390 resistor (orange white brown gold) from hole C6 to hole J6.
8. R10 100k resistor (brown black yellow gold) from hole F8 to hole J8.
9. R4 22k resistor (red red orange gold) from hole A10 to hole E10.
10. R6 33k resistor (orange orange orange gold) from hole G11 to hole J11.
11. R7 470k resistor (yellow violet yellow gold) from hole A11 to hole D11.
12. R9 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole C12 to hole J12.
13. R8 470k resistor (yellow violet yellow gold) from hole D14 to hole J14.
14. L1 500mH inductor from hole B7 to hole G7.
15. C1 10nF film capacitor from hole F2 to hole D2.
16. C2 220nF film capacitor from hole E8 to hole B8.
17. C4 10nF film capacitor from hole H13 to hole D13.
18. Q1 2N5088 (TO-92, pins EBC left to right): E at C3, B at D3, C at E3. Polarity check: flat face toward the left.
19. Q2 2N5088 (TO-92, pins EBC left to right): E at C14, B at B14, C at A14. Polarity check: flat face toward the right.
20. C3 4.7µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole C9, - at hole G9. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole G9.
21. C5 4.7µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole C16, - at hole F16. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole F16.
22. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: GND pad at hole J1 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground; +9V pad at hole A4 to the DC jack + (the sleeve lug on a 2.1 mm centre-negative jack) or the battery clip +; IN pad at hole F1 to the input jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); OUT pad at hole F10 to the output jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); RV1 Sweep pot (C100k, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to B5, lug 2 to H9, lug 3 to J9.
23. Before power: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between +9V (A4) and GND (J1). It must not read near 0 ohms. Then check the neighbouring strips at every cut for solder bridges.

Notes:
- C3 sits with almost no DC across it, so its polarity hardly matters; the layout puts + toward e1.
