RC treble-cut filter

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 4 holes wide by 5 strips (0.4 x 0.5 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to E from the top and holes numbered 1 to 4 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. No track cuts are needed.
3. R1 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole D2 to hole A2.
4. R2 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole A3 to hole E3.
5. C1 10nF film capacitor from hole A4 to hole E4.
6. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: OUT pad at hole A1 to the output jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); IN pad at hole D1 to the input jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); GND pad at hole E1 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground.
7. Before connecting anything: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between neighbouring strips at every cut, and that IN and OUT do not read near 0 ohms to GND.
