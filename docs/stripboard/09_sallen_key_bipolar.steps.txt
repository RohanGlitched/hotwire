Sallen-Key low-pass on +/-9 V

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 14 holes wide by 9 strips (1.4 x 0.9 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to I from the top and holes numbered 1 to 14 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. Cut the copper at 8 holes: B4, D9, E6, E9, F5, F9, G9, I9. On the copper side left and right are mirrored, so count from the same corner. Twist a 3.5 mm drill bit by hand until the copper ring is gone, then hold the board to a light to make sure no sliver bridges the cut.
3. Fit 5 wire links (insulated solid-core wire, lying flat): C7 to E7, B6 to F6, A12 to E12, C13 to F13, E14 to I14.
4. R1 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole B1 to hole E1.
5. R0 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole F2 to hole I2.
6. R3 100 resistor (brown black brown gold) from hole C4 to hole H4.
7. R2 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole E5 to hole B5.
8. U1 8-pin DIP socket with pin 1 at hole D8, notch toward the top of the board. The chip goes in last.
9. C1 22nF film capacitor from hole E2 to hole C2.
10. C2 10nF film capacitor from hole F7 to hole I7.
11. C0 1µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole B3, - at hole F3. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole F3.
12. C4 10µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole I3, - at hole G3. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole G3.
13. C3 10µF electrolytic, 16 V or more lying along the strip over the cut: + at hole I10, - at hole I8. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole I8.
14. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: +9V pad at hole A1 to the DC jack + (the sleeve lug on a 2.1 mm centre-negative jack) or the battery clip +; GND pad at hole I1 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground; IN pad at hole F1 to the input jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); OUT pad at hole H1 to the output jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); −9V pad at hole G1 to the negative supply.
15. Before power: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between +9V (A1) and GND (I1). It must not read near 0 ohms. Then check the neighbouring strips at every cut for solder bridges.
16. Fit the chip: TL071 into the U1 socket, notch matching. Power up and measure the supply at the V+ pin (U1 pin 7) before playing.

Notes:
- C0 sits with almost no DC across it, so its polarity hardly matters; the layout puts + toward a.
