Op-amp gain stage with diode clipper

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 17 holes wide by 11 strips (1.7 x 1.1 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to K from the top and holes numbered 1 to 17 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. Cut the copper at 8 holes: B4, B8, C8, C12, D8, E8, E12, I9. On the copper side left and right are mirrored, so count from the same corner. Twist a 3.5 mm drill bit by hand until the copper ring is gone, then hold the board to a light to make sure no sliver bridges the cut.
3. Fit 6 wire links (insulated solid-core wire, lying flat): F6 to B6, I5 to C5, K4 to E4, A11 to B11, C11 to D11, G11 to E11.
4. R3 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole D1 to hole G1.
5. R1 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole A3 to hole G3.
6. R7 100k resistor (brown black yellow gold) from hole H3 to hole K3.
7. R4 4.7k resistor (yellow violet red gold) from hole I8 to hole I11 lying along the strip over the cut.
8. R2 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole G13 to hole K13.
9. R5 1k resistor (brown black red gold) from hole F13 to hole C13.
10. R6 1k resistor (brown black red gold) from hole J15 to hole E15.
11. D1 1N4148 diode: anode at hole E14, cathode at hole K14. Polarity check: the band goes at hole K14.
12. D2 1N4148 diode: anode at hole K16, cathode at hole E16. Polarity check: the band goes at hole E16.
13. U1 8-pin DIP socket with pin 1 at hole B7, notch toward the top of the board. The chip goes in last.
14. C4 100pF ceramic capacitor from hole F2 to hole I2.
15. C2 100nF film capacitor from hole B2 to hole D2.
16. C1 47µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole G7, - at hole K7. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole K7.
17. C5 1µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole F12, - at hole J12. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole J12.
18. C3 1µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole I17, - at hole K17. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole K17.
19. C6 1µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole H17, - at hole E17. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole E17.
20. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: GND pad at hole K1 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground; +9V pad at hole A1 to the DC jack + (the sleeve lug on a 2.1 mm centre-negative jack) or the battery clip +; IN pad at hole B1 to the input jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); OUT pad at hole H10 to the output jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); RV1 Gain pot (A500k, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to C14, lug 2 to I1, lug 3 to I1.
21. Before power: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between +9V (A1) and GND (K1). It must not read near 0 ohms. Then check the neighbouring strips at every cut for solder bridges.
22. Fit the chip: TL072 into the U1 socket, notch matching. Power up and measure the supply at the V+ pin (U1 pin 8) before playing.

Notes:
- RV1 lugs 2 and 3 share a net: join them on the pot with a short wire and run one wire to the RV1·2+3 pad.
- C6 sits with almost no DC across it, so its polarity hardly matters; the layout puts + toward out.
- Only one half of U1 (TL072) is used, so the spare half is tied off safely: pin 5 (IN+ B) to vref and pin 7 (OUT B) to pin 6 (IN- B) with a link, making it an idle follower.
