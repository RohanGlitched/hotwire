JFET input buffer

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 8 holes wide by 9 strips (0.8 x 0.9 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to I from the top and holes numbered 1 to 8 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. No track cuts are needed.
3. R3 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole A1 to hole F1.
4. R1 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole E3 to hole I3.
5. R4 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole F4 to hole I4.
6. R2 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole C6 to hole F6.
7. R5 3.3k resistor (orange orange red gold) from hole B7 to hole I7.
8. R6 100k resistor (brown black yellow gold) from hole D8 to hole I8.
9. C1 100nF film capacitor from hole E2 to hole C2.
10. J1 J201 (TO-92, pins DSG left to right): D at A4, S at B4, G at C4. Polarity check: flat face toward the left.
11. C2 10µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole F2, - at hole I2. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole I2.
12. C3 1µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole B3, - at hole D3. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole D3.
13. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: GND pad at hole I1 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground; +9V pad at hole A2 to the DC jack + (the sleeve lug on a 2.1 mm centre-negative jack) or the battery clip +; IN pad at hole E4 to the input jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); OUT pad at hole D4 to the output jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass).
14. Before power: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between +9V (A2) and GND (I1). It must not read near 0 ohms. Then check the neighbouring strips at every cut for solder bridges.
