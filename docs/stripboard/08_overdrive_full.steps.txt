Full overdrive pedal board

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 27 holes wide by 14 strips (2.7 x 1.4 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to N from the top and holes numbered 1 to 27 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. Cut the copper at 12 holes: F16, G7, H7, J9, K12, K17, L12, L17, L22, M17, N17, N21. On the copper side left and right are mirrored, so count from the same corner. Twist a 3.5 mm drill bit by hand until the copper ring is gone, then hold the board to a light to make sure no sliver bridges the cut.
3. Fit 8 wire links (insulated solid-core wire, lying flat): B2 to H2, L9 to I9, N11 to J11, D15 to K15, A14 to L14, I20 to K20, E21 to L21, L20 to M20.
4. R4 1M resistor (brown black green gold) from hole G4 to hole N4.
5. R5 510k resistor (green brown yellow gold) from hole J5 to hole F5.
6. R1 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole L6 to hole F6.
7. R6 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole K10 to hole N10.
8. R2 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole F12 to hole J12.
9. R7 510k resistor (green brown yellow gold) from hole M13 to hole F13.
10. R9 51k resistor (green brown orange gold) from hole D18 to hole G18.
11. R8 4.7k resistor (yellow violet red gold) from hole A20 to hole F20.
12. R11 1k resistor (brown black red gold) from hole N23 to hole N20 lying along the strip over the cut.
13. R10 1k resistor (brown black red gold) from hole D23 to hole H23.
14. R12 100k resistor (brown black yellow gold) from hole C25 to hole J25.
15. R3 4.7k resistor (yellow violet red gold) from hole I27 to hole L27.
16. D1 1N5817 diode: anode at hole H1, cathode at hole L1. Polarity check: the band goes at hole L1.
17. D4 1N4148 diode: anode at hole A12, cathode at hole D12. Polarity check: the band goes at hole D12.
18. D3 1N4148 diode: anode at hole D17, cathode at hole A17. Polarity check: the band goes at hole A17.
19. U1 8-pin DIP socket with pin 1 at hole K16, notch toward the top of the board. The chip goes in last.
20. C7 51pF ceramic capacitor from hole D16 to hole A16.
21. C2 100nF film capacitor from hole L2 to hole N2.
22. C4 22nF film capacitor from hole G3 to hole J3.
23. C8 220nF film capacitor from hole H22 to hole J22.
24. C6 47nF film capacitor from hole F24 to hole J24.
25. C9 100nF film capacitor from hole N26 to hole J26.
26. Q1 2N5088 (TO-92, pins EBC left to right): E at K8, B at J8, C at I8. Polarity check: flat face toward the right.
27. C1 100µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole L3, - at hole N3. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole N3.
28. C5 1µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole M5, - at hole K5. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole K5.
29. C3 47µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole F10, - at hole J10. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole J10.
30. C10 10µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole E13, - at hole C13. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole C13.
31. D2 red LED: anode (long leg) at hole L23, cathode (short leg, flat side) at hole J23. Polarity check: flat side toward hole J23.
32. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: GND pad at hole N1 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground; +9V pad at hole B1 to the DC jack + (the sleeve lug on a 2.1 mm centre-negative jack) or the battery clip +; IN pad at hole G1 to the input jack tip (through the footswitch for true bypass); RV1 Drive pot (A500k, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to G19, lug 2 to A22, lug 3 to A22; RV2 Tone pot (B20k, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to H18, lug 2 to N24, lug 3 to F8; RV3 Level pot (A100k, shaft toward you, lugs down, lug 1 on the left): lug 1 to C5, lug 2 to the output jack tip, lug 3 to N5.
33. Before power: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between +9V (B1) and GND (N1). It must not read near 0 ohms. Then check the neighbouring strips at every cut for solder bridges.
34. Fit the chip: TL072 into the U1 socket, notch matching. Power up and measure the supply at the V+ pin (U1 pin 8) before playing.

Notes:
- RV1 lugs 2 and 3 share a net: join them on the pot with a short wire and run one wire to the RV1·2+3 pad.
- Nothing on the board touches net out: wire the output jack tip and RV3 lug 2 straight to each other.
