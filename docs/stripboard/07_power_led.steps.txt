Power section with LED and reverse-polarity diode

1. Cut a piece of stripboard 10 holes wide by 6 strips (1.0 x 0.6 in), strips running left to right. Strips are lettered A to F from the top and holes numbered 1 to 10 from the left, so hole C12 is strip C, hole 12. The layout is drawn from the component side.
2. No track cuts are needed.
3. R3 4.7k resistor (yellow violet red gold) from hole A4 to hole E4.
4. R1 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole A8 to hole F8.
5. R2 10k resistor (brown black orange gold) from hole F9 to hole C9.
6. D1 1N5817 diode: anode at hole D1, cathode at hole A1. Polarity check: the band goes at hole A1.
7. C2 100nF film capacitor from hole A3 to hole C3.
8. C1 100µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole A2, - at hole C2. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole C2.
9. C3 47µF electrolytic, 16 V or more: + at hole F10, - at hole C10. Polarity check: the stripe (-) faces hole C10.
10. D2 red LED: anode (long leg) at hole E5, cathode (short leg, flat side) at hole C5. Polarity check: flat side toward hole C5.
11. Off-board wiring, stranded wire about 10 cm long: +9V pad at hole D2 to the DC jack + (the sleeve lug on a 2.1 mm centre-negative jack) or the battery clip +; GND pad at hole C6 to the jack sleeves and the DC jack ground.
12. Before power: with a meter on ohms, check for shorts between +9V (D2) and GND (C6). It must not read near 0 ohms. Then check the neighbouring strips at every cut for solder bridges.
